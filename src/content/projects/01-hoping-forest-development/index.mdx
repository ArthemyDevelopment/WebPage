## Origins of the project

This project was born on may 2022 during the game jam "Mini-Jam 106: Frogs2", a bi-weekly jam hosted on itch.io with a pre announced theme and a limitiation revealed at the start of the jam. The theme was "Frogs" and the limitation was "Limited time".
At that time I wanted to make a puzzle game and got inspiration by the hacking minigame of Bioshock and decided to develop this connect-path puzzle game, using the theme of the jam for the protagonist and the limitation for the maximum movements allowed.

The first version of the project had a very simple and flat aesthetic, without any sounds and consisted of 5 levels and was called "Why the Frog cross the road".
